Think of it like the difference between a frantic metronome and a slow ocean wave. stillpoint doesn't rush you. It doesn't scream for attention. It simply shows you where things stand — calmly, clearly, and with a touch of visual elegance.

## 💆 Why Choose stillpoint?

### 1. Visual Comfort
Most progress bars use harsh, blocky characters that grate on the eyes. stillpoint uses a soft gradient fill with a gentle leading edge. The colors blend smoothly, making long waits easier to watch.

### 2. Steady Pace
Other progress tools refresh dozens of times per second, creating flicker and motion sickness. stillpoint paces its movement with an easing curve — the bar moves at a human-friendly speed, not your CPU's speed. You can actually follow it with your eyes.

Progress labels aren't just functional text — stillpoint treats them like a printed page. The font spacing, sizing, and alignment are carefully crafted. Reading "training 34 – 40 88% 0:04" feels like reading a well-set line from a book, not a wall of machine output.

## 🛠️ How to Use stillpoint

Using stillpoint is deliberately simple. Once you open it, you'll see a small window with a progress bar and a label area. Here's what you can do:

### Starting a Task
1. Click the **"New Task"** button (the plus icon).
2. Give your task a name (like "Backup" or "Render Video").
3. Choose the total amount of work (e.g., 100 steps, or 500 files, or 10 minutes).
4. Press **Start**.

### Watching Progress
The bar will begin filling at a calm, steady pace. The label shows:
- The task name (left side)
- Current progress (middle)
- Percentage and time remaining (right side)

You don't need to do anything — just watch. Or don't watch. The bar doesn't demand your attention.

### Pausing or Canceling
- **Pause:** Hit the pause button (two vertical lines) to freeze the task.
- **Cancel:** Hit the X button to stop and discard the task.
- **Reset:** After a task finishes, hit the reset icon to run it again with the same settings.

### Using Breathe Mode
If you're waiting for something unpredictable (like a file download), switch to Breathe mode by clicking the moon icon. The bar will change to a soft pulsing animation — like a gentle breath in and out — without any fake numbers. When the task actually completes, you'll see a quiet checkmark.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting (If Something Goes Wrong)

Most issues are rare, but here's what to do:
